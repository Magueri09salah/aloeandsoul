# Summary
Aloe vera blended with peach purée and white grape juice concentrate for a mellow, smooth taste. Rich in vitamin C, it is an ideal drink when you feel occasional tiredness.

# Description
The new Aloe Peaches formula combines aloe vera (84.5%), peach purée and white grape juice concentrate for a mellow, tasty flavour, with a synergistic dose of vitamin C. The packaging is 100% recyclable. Discover the brand-new Aloe Peaches for a healthy, delicious break!

# Benefits
Aloe Peaches combines aloe vera gel with natural peach purée, white grape juice concentrate and vitamin C for everyday well-being.

Aloe vera contains more than 250 essential components (vitamins, minerals, amino acids, sugars, lipids and glycoproteins) that support everyday well-being. Aloe vera is known in particular for its beneficial effect in times of tiredness, and helps increase resistance to mental strain caused by stress.

The formula is enriched with vitamin C, which is also naturally present in aloe vera. Vitamin C is recognised for reducing tiredness and supporting good energy levels.

# How to use
Take 40 ml diluted in 240 ml of water or juice, 3 times a day. Shake before use.

**Precautions**

Store in a cool, dry place away from light. Do not exceed the recommended daily dose. Do not use if the seal under the cap is broken or missing. Keep out of reach of young children. Our products are intended for healthy people, to help keep balance and well-being, and can never replace medical treatment.
