# Summary
Aloe-Jojoba Shampoo gently cleanses and hydrates all hair types. Aloe vera and jojoba oil leave hair supple and shiny and soothe the scalp.

# Description
Aloe-Jojoba Shampoo is designed to cleanse deeply and hydrate all hair types, leaving your hair hydrated, shiny, healthy and soft. By combining pure aloe vera with jojoba oil, which strengthens hair, Forever's new Aloe-Jojoba Shampoo formula is perfect for daily use and brings out the best in your hair. With no added sulphates, it helps maintain the scalp's moisture balance while keeping it soothed.

# Benefits
Helps soften hair and makes it easier to style.

Aloe-Jojoba Shampoo combines the best of science and nature for clean, shiny, healthy hair.

# How to use
Apply to wet hair, lather and rinse thoroughly. Follow with Aloe-Jojoba Conditioner.

**Precautions**

In case of contact with the eyes, rinse with clean water for several minutes. For external use only.
