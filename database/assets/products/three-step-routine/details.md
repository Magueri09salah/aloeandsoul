# Summary
The Logic by Forever routine is a three-step daily routine (a cleanser, an essence and a moisturiser) designed to cleanse, tone and hydrate the skin. Each product is made with ingredients of natural origin.

# Description
**Start a new beauty routine with the Logic by Forever kit**: a smart three-step approach designed to gently cleanse, rebalance and hydrate. Each formula draws its strength from ingredients of natural origin, with our pure aloe vera gel, hand-harvested in Forever's own fields, at its heart.

The Logic by Forever routine includes:

- the **Gel Cleanser**
- the **Balancing Essence**
- the **Gel Moisturiser**

Designed for all skin types, including sensitive and combination skin, this daily routine works best when the three products are used together. With no added fragrance, parabens or silicones, each clean formula also fits perfectly into your Forever skincare ritual.

At the heart of each product is aloe vera gel from the inner leaf, known for its soothing, hydrating and protective properties. It is extracted and stabilised within six hours using an exclusive Forever process that keeps it fresh and effective.

### The three products

**Logic by Forever aloe gel cleanser (#667, 88 ml)**: a gentle but effective cleanser made with more than 96% ingredients of natural origin. It combines aloe vera, jojoba esters and desert date extract to purify and cleanse without irritating.

**Logic by Forever balancing aloe essence (#668, 73 ml)**: with more than 98% ingredients of natural origin, this essence rebalances the skin while hydrating it, thanks to aloe vera, cucumber extract and green tea.

**Logic by Forever soothing gel moisturizer (#669, 59 ml)**: made with more than 94% ingredients of natural origin, this light gel combines aloe vera, niacinamide and baobab seed extract to nourish, soothe and hydrate without a greasy finish.

# How to use
Use every day, in this order:

1. Cleanse with the Gel Cleanser.
2. Tone with the Balancing Essence.
3. Hydrate with the Gel Moisturiser.
