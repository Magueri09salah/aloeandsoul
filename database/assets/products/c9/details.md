# Description
Choose vanilla or chocolate flavour. C9 is the first step of the Forever F.I.T. slimming and fitness programme. It lasts 9 days and is designed to cleanse the body by eliminating toxins. Results appear from the first days: weight loss, a feeling of lightness and renewed energy.

The pack contains:

- 2 bottles of Forever Aloe Vera Gel
- 18 Forever Therm tablets
- 36 Forever Lean capsules
- 9 Forever Fiber sticks
- 1 pouch of Forever Lite Ultra, vanilla or chocolate
- 1 guide booklet (digital)

# Benefits
C9 is a short, easy programme. In just 9 days it detoxifies the body, helps you slim down and, above all, helps you get back to good eating habits. Free of the toxins and bad habits that slow it down, the body regains energy and vitality.

C9 is also a complete programme, with day-by-day guidance in the booklet included in the pack: advice on nutrition and on the physical activity to do during the 9 days. Followed carefully, the programme gives the best results.

# How to use
Follow the instructions in the programme booklet.

**Duration:** 9 days.

**Precautions**

Store the food supplements in a cool, dry place away from light. Keep the aloe vera gel in the fridge after opening. The C9 programme is not recommended for children or for pregnant or breastfeeding women. Keep out of reach of young children.
