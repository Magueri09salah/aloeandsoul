# Summary
A tasty, nutritious snack to help you keep in shape. It can complete a light meal by providing vitamins, minerals, protein and carbohydrates.

# Description
To help you keep in shape, this tasty, nutritious snack can complete a light meal by providing vitamins, minerals, protein and carbohydrates. Forever Lite Ultra Chocolate contributes to the maintenance of muscle mass and to normal energy-yielding metabolism.

# Benefits
**Soy protein isolate**

Soy protein is the only complete plant protein: it contains all the essential amino acids, which the body cannot make and must get from food. As part of a healthy, balanced diet, it helps prevent the loss of muscle that can happen when protein intake is too low. Soy protein also contains isoflavones, which have antioxidant properties.

**Plant enzyme blend**

These plant-based enzymes support better digestion of the protein you take in.

**Vitamins, minerals and trace elements**

Tiredness, low mood and other discomforts can occur during any slimming programme. Vitamins B1, B2, B3, B5, B6, B8, B9, B12, C, D and E and the minerals copper, magnesium, zinc and iron contribute to normal energy-yielding metabolism, help reduce tiredness and support the body's natural defences.

# How to use
Mix one 25 g scoop into 150 to 300 ml of skimmed milk or another drink (plant milk, water). Shake well before drinking.

**Precautions**

Keep tightly closed in a cool, dry place away from light. Do not use if the packaging is pierced. Maintain an adequate daily fluid intake. Forever Lite Ultra Chocolate is a food supplement: use it as part of a healthy lifestyle, not as a substitute for a varied and balanced diet.
