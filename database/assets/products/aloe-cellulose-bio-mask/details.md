# Summary
New formula. This mask of cellulose fibres soaked in a dedicated serum is the result of a unique technological innovation, for skin that is soothed, soft, perfectly hydrated and regenerated.

# Description
Beautiful smooth, supple and elastic skin is above all well-hydrated skin. Our skin is 70% water, and its moisture balance is essential. Thanks to a brand-new technology, the Forever Aloe Bio-Cellulose Mask soothes, hydrates and regenerates the skin, leaving it plumped, radiant and smooth.

# Benefits
The mask, soaked in a serum made specifically for it, is the result of a world-first technological innovation that took 18 months to develop. It is made of pure cellulose fibres, 100% natural, 100% biodegradable and 100% biocompatible, produced by bacteria from fermented coconut water. The bacteria are "fed" with Forever aloe vera and seaweed, and produce cellulose fibres 1,000 times finer than a hair, whose 3D structure adheres perfectly to the contours of the skin.

The mask is then infused with a Forever serum created for it and enriched with:

- **aloe vera**, which soothes, hydrates and regenerates the skin
- **horse chestnut**, which tones, refreshes tired-looking skin and adds radiance
- **glycerin**, which softens and hydrates
- **green tea and antioxidants**, for anti-ageing care

Together, the mask and serum offer a true "bath of active ingredients", with far better penetration than ordinary masks. The result is visible immediately and builds with each use.

The Bio-Cellulose Mask is 100% compostable and can be put in garden soil or a plant pot. Forever works with a mask factory certified LEED (Leadership in Energy and Environmental Design), recognised worldwide for reducing its environmental footprint.

# How to use
Use 3 times a month. Leave on for 20 minutes.

**Precautions**

Store in a cool, dry place away from light. Use immediately after opening. The mask is compostable: please do not leave it in nature.
