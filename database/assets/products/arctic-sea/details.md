### About fatty acids

Fats have always been part of the human diet. While some fats are not good for health, others are essential to the body. Not knowing this family of nutrients well leads us to eat less and less of them, and so to cut down on some fats that are essential for health.

**What are fatty acids?**

Lipids are made of two main families of fatty acids:

- **Saturated fatty acids**, found mainly in animal fats such as meat or cheese, in plant fats and, more and more, in industrial ready meals. These fats are often discouraged because, eaten in excess, they bring "bad" cholesterol.
- **Unsaturated fatty acids**, in two groups: monounsaturated fats such as omega-9, found mainly in olive oil, and polyunsaturated fats, the omega-3s and omega-6s. Omega-3s are found in some vegetable oils (olive, rapeseed, hemp, linseed…) and especially in oily fish (salmon, mackerel, herring…). These unsaturated fatty acids, essential to health, are called "essential" because the human body cannot make them.

**What do omega-3s do?**

Among the omega-3s are two particular fatty acids, EPA (eicosapentaenoic acid) and DHA (docosahexaenoic acid), found in oily fish, vegetable oils and some green vegetables. The body cannot make them, so they must be provided every day through food or supplements. They are vital for health, in particular for the normal function of the brain and heart, and for vision.

**The active ingredients**

The new Forever Arctic Sea formula contains more omega-3s, with much more EPA (2.5 times more) and DHA (3.7 times more) thanks to fish and squid oils.

- **Fish oil standardised in omega-3**: marine fats are "good fats" that contain plenty of EPA and DHA and so contribute to normal heart function. Forever Arctic Sea is a rich source, for a very good omega-3 intake.
- **Squid oil standardised in omega-3**: squid oil is high in omega-3s, especially DHA. The new formula has a boosted dose of DHA from squid oil, which supports normal brain and heart function and the maintenance of normal vision.
- **Olive oil standardised in oleic acid**: olive oil is very rich in oleic acid, a monounsaturated fatty acid.

# How to use
**When:** preferably with meals.

Take 2 capsules 3 times a day, that is 6 capsules a day.

**Precautions**

Not recommended for pregnant or breastfeeding women. Seek advice from a healthcare professional before taking this product. Food supplements should be used as part of a healthy lifestyle, not as a substitute for a varied and balanced diet. Do not exceed the recommended daily dose. Keep out of reach of children.
