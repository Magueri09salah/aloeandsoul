# Summary
Aloe Body Lotion soothes, hydrates and protects the skin from dryness. Light and non-greasy, it absorbs quickly and leaves skin supple, soft and smoother-looking. Silicone-free; suitable for sensitive skin and after sun.

# Description
Aloe Body Lotion soothes, hydrates and protects the skin from dryness.

You can use it all year round, and as an after-sun in summer: its soothing aloe vera calms redness caused by too much heat and leaves a feeling of freshness and comfort. Its gel texture melts into the skin at once, delivering all the benefits of our pure aloe vera and its hydrating actives.

It is enriched with hydrating agents such as aloe vera (37.7%) and jojoba esters, rich in vitamins, trace elements and minerals, which protect the skin and keep it hydrated.

It also contains a complex of replenishing actives, such as argan oil with its restorative properties, which soothe and soften the skin.

Regenerating, nourishing and repairing, macadamia seed oil, with its high palmitoleic acid content, helps give the skin a smoother look. Its antioxidants also help protect the skin, notably against the effects of UV rays, and help fight skin ageing.

A fresh, delicate fragrance. Silicone-free. Suitable for sensitive skin.

# Benefits
- Enriched with hydrating agents such as aloe vera and jojoba esters, rich in vitamins, trace elements and minerals, which protect the skin and keep it hydrated.
- Contains a complex of replenishing actives such as argan oil, whose restorative properties soothe and soften the skin.
- Regenerating, nourishing and repairing, macadamia seed oil, with its high palmitoleic acid content, helps give the skin a smoother look. Its antioxidants also help protect the skin, notably against the effects of UV rays, and help fight skin ageing.
- Absorbs quickly, leaving a silky, non-greasy feel.

# How to use
Apply to clean, dry skin all over the body. Massage until fully absorbed.

**Precautions**

In case of contact with the eyes, rinse with clean water for several minutes. For external use only.
