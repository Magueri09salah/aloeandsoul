# Summary
A generous dose of aloe vera with a hint of apple and cranberry juice. Rich in vitamin C, it provides a synergistic dose of antioxidants that help protect cells against oxidative stress.

# Description
A large dose (90.7%) of aloe vera gel, a hint of apple and cranberry juice, vitamin C, no preservatives and 100% recyclable packaging: that is the secret of the brand-new Aloe Berry Nectar formula. Our aloe vera at the heart of a tangy formula, for healthy enjoyment and the same everyday well-being.

# Benefits
Aloe Berry Nectar combines aloe vera gel with apple and cranberry juice and vitamin C for everyday well-being.

Aloe vera contains more than 250 essential components (vitamins, minerals, amino acids, sugars, lipids and glycoproteins) that support everyday well-being. Aloe vera is known in particular for its beneficial effect in times of tiredness, and helps increase resistance to mental strain caused by stress.

The formula is enriched with vitamin C, which is also naturally present in aloe vera. Vitamin C is recognised for reducing tiredness and supporting good energy levels.

# How to use
Take 40 ml diluted in 240 ml of water or juice, 3 times a day. Shake before use.

**Precautions**

Use as part of a healthy lifestyle, not as a substitute for a varied and balanced diet. After opening, keep in the fridge (7 °C maximum) and drink within 30 days.

Stabilised aloe vera gel [aloe vera gel (90.7%), vitamin C (ascorbic acid), antioxidant (ascorbic acid), acidifier (citric acid)], natural apple juice concentrate (4%), fructose, natural cranberry juice concentrate (2%).
