# Summary
Vitality, strength, endurance, extreme sport: Forever ARGI+ combines L-arginine with a unique blend of vitamins and fruit extracts to support you during intense exercise.

# Description
Vitality, strength, endurance, extreme sport: Forever ARGI+ is a food supplement made with L-arginine, ribose, vitamins and plants, with sugars and sweeteners, to support you during and after exercise.

Vitamins C, B6 and B9 contribute to normal energy-yielding metabolism and to normal glycogen metabolism. They also help to reduce tiredness and fatigue.

# Benefits
Forever ARGI+ combines L-arginine with a unique blend of vitamins and fruit extracts. Each serving provides 5 g of L-arginine together with vitamins to give the body tone and endurance.

- **L-arginine** is an amino acid with several important roles in the body: it supports cell division and the building of proteins, and helps the body recover after prolonged exercise.
- **Vitamin C** is essential to maintaining the normal function of the immune system during and after intense physical exercise.
- **Vitamins B6 and B12** and **folic acid** help the body cope with the stress of competition by contributing to energy-yielding metabolism and reducing tiredness.

Mix it with water, or with aloe vera gel for an extra boost: you will love the forest-fruit taste of ARGI+.

Contains no lactose or gluten.

# How to use
Mix one sachet into 240 ml of a drink (water, fruit juice or aloe vera gel).

**Precautions**

Not recommended for pregnant or breastfeeding women. Do not give to children. Store in a cool, dry place away from light. Use as part of a healthy lifestyle, not as a substitute for a varied and balanced diet. Do not exceed the recommended daily dose. Keep out of reach of children. Maintain an adequate daily fluid intake.
