# Summary
The Eye Contour Cream is the secret to a brighter look. It combines aloe vera with a collagen extract for visibly younger-looking eyes.

# Description
Discover the secret to a radiant look! The Eye Contour Cream takes a complete approach to eye-area care: it visibly lifts the eyelids and reduces puffiness and dark circles thanks to a complex of patented active ingredients. It goes further by visibly smoothing crow's feet and fine lines. The result: eyes that look rested and radiant.

# Benefits
The Eye Contour Cream takes a complete approach to eye-area care, for a fresh, lifted and brighter look. It works in two ways:

- **It visibly lifts the eyelids and reduces puffiness and dark circles** thanks to patented active ingredients with tested and proven effectiveness. Among them is B-Eye 1, a synergistic combination of unique ingredients such as silk tree and Siegesbeckia ("divine herb") extracts, which help strengthen micro-circulation around the eye.
- **It visibly smooths crow's feet and fine lines** thanks to collagen of marine origin. A second patented active, B-Eye 2, combines several powerful ingredients (hyaluronic acid, Dunaliella salina extract, a tripeptide…) for a unique smoothing action.

# How to use
**When:** morning and evening.

Apply in small dabs around the eyes, patting gently.
