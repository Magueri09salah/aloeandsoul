# Description
Made from whole leaves harvested and filleted by hand, Forever Aloe Vera Gel is 99.7% aloe vera. Aloe vera contributes to the normal function of the immune system. A source of antioxidants, it protects cells and tissues from oxidation. Rich in vitamin C, this formula helps reduce tiredness and supports normal energy-yielding metabolism.

Now in a 100% recyclable PET bottle.

# Benefits
This new aloe vera gel formula brings together all the benefits of the plant with those of vitamin C. Aloe vera contains more than 250 essential components (vitamins, minerals, amino acids, sugars, lipids and glycoproteins) that support everyday well-being. Aloe vera is known in particular for strengthening the body's natural defences and stimulating metabolism.

The formula is enriched with vitamin C, which is also naturally present in the plant. Vitamin C is recognised for supporting the normal function of the immune system and reducing tiredness.

# How to use
Take 40 ml diluted in 240 ml of water or juice, 3 times a day. Shake before use.

**Precautions**

Use as part of a healthy lifestyle, not as a substitute for a varied and balanced diet. After opening, keep in the fridge (7 °C maximum) and drink within 30 days.
