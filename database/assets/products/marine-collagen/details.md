### Questions and answers

**Is the fish in Forever Marine Collagen sustainably sourced?**

The tilapia used in Forever Marine Collagen comes from sustainable fishing. The fish are subject to strict quality controls, including quotas, catch seasons and catch methods, enforced by the relevant government bodies: Fisheries and Oceans Canada, NOAA Fisheries (United States) and the Common Fisheries Policy (Europe).

**Does it contain crustaceans or shellfish?**

No, Forever Marine Collagen contains no crustaceans or shellfish.

**Does it contain shark collagen?**

No, the collagen does not come from sharks.

**What type of collagen does it contain?**

Mainly type I collagen peptides, with smaller amounts of type II, III, IV, V, VI, VII, VIII, IX and X collagen peptides.

**How do I take it? Can I mix it with other drinks?**

You can drink the sachet as it is, or dilute it in water or another drink.

**How long should I take it?**

Collagen breaks down every day and needs help to rebuild. For the best results, add Forever Marine Collagen to your daily routine and keep taking it every day.

**What is the best time of day to take collagen?**

It depends on your preferences and your schedule. Some people prefer the morning on an empty stomach to maximise absorption; others prefer the evening, giving the body time to process the collagen during sleep. Whichever you choose, take it at the same time each day.

**What does it taste like?**

It tastes really good, with a tangy exotic-fruit flavour.

**Do I need to change my supplement or skincare routine?**

No. Simply enjoy one sachet of Forever Marine Collagen at the same time each day.

**Is it GMO-free and gluten-free?**

Yes, Forever Marine Collagen is GMO-free and gluten-free.

**Is it suitable for vegans or vegetarians?**

No, because it contains fish.

**How many calories, sugars and protein does it contain?**

Each 15 ml sachet contains 30 calories, 2 g of total sugars and 4 g of protein.
