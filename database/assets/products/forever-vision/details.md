# Summary
Forever iVision contains vitamin A and zinc, which contribute to the maintenance of normal vision.

# Description
Adapt your eyes to a connected lifestyle! Our eyes are under constant demand from screens, so it is important to protect them against long-term damage. Forever iVision is the food supplement for your vision, providing vitamin A and zinc.

# Benefits
iVision contains the concentrated power of vitamins, antioxidants and plant ingredients.

- Vitamin A and zinc contribute to the maintenance of normal vision. Vitamins C and E and zinc help protect cells against oxidative stress. Bilberry, a source of vitamin C and very rich in vitamin A, is an antioxidant, plays a part in healthy retinal function and may have a beneficial effect on eye fatigue.
- Lutemax 2020®, recognised worldwide and winner of a NutrAward in 2018, is a valuable aid to vision. It combines three important carotenoids (lutein, zeaxanthin and meso-zeaxanthin) that the body does not make, which protect the eyes against oxidative stress caused by the many kinds of light around us.

# How to use
Take 2 capsules a day with half a glass of water.

**Precautions**

Beta-carotene is not recommended for smokers. Keep out of reach of young children. Do not exceed the recommended daily dose. Food supplements should not be used as a substitute for a healthy lifestyle and a varied, balanced diet. Store in a cool, dry place, away from sunlight and light.
