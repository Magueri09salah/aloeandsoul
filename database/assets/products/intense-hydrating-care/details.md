# Summary
This regenerating cream works like a bath of hydration and gives the skin extreme comfort. Skin is plumped, smoothed and looks denser; wrinkles and fine lines are visibly reduced.

# Description
This regenerating cream works like a bath of hydration and keeps the skin extremely comfortable all day long.

Enriched with aloe vera gel and macadamia and sunflower seed oils, it strengthens the skin barrier and leaves the skin soft and comfortable, thanks to a rich texture that melts gently into the skin.

The skin is immediately intensely hydrated, nourished and looks better. Day after day, wrinkles and skin texture are visibly smoothed, the skin is plumped and the complexion is brighter. The skin is revitalised.

# Benefits
- A regenerating cream that deeply hydrates and nourishes the skin
- An immediate feeling of comfort
- Contains macadamia and sunflower seed oils
- A rich, creamy texture
- Ideal for dry to very dry, dehydrated skin

# How to use
**When:** morning and evening.

Dot the cream on the cheeks, forehead and chin, then massage gently until fully absorbed.
