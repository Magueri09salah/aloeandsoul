# Summary
Alcohol-free, aluminium-salt-free and lightly fragranced, this deodorant gives effective protection without staining your clothes. Its aloe vera formula softens and hydrates the skin.

# Description
The Aloe Ever-Shield deodorant stick prevents unwanted body odour while protecting the skin from drying out, so you stay fresh all day long. Its formula, free of aluminium salts and alcohol, does not cause irritation. Lightly fragranced, it suits both women and men, and its texture does not stain clothes.

# Benefits
**Key ingredient**

**Stabilised aloe vera gel**: a powerful moisturiser that the skin absorbs quickly. Rich in vitamins, minerals and polysaccharides, it helps hydrate and protect the skin.

# How to use
**When:** in the morning or before exercise.

Apply to clean, dry skin. As it is alcohol-free, it can be applied after hair removal.

**Precautions**

For external use only.
