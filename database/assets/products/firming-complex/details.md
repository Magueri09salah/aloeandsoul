# Summary
The Firming Complex works from within against visible signs of ageing.

# Description
The Firming Complex is one of the four products in the Infinite by Forever range. This nutraceutical food supplement works from within against visible signs of ageing. It gives the body the nutrients it needs to redensify the skin from within, bringing it flexibility and hydration.

# Benefits
**The Firming Complex** is a nutraceutical food supplement: it cares for the skin and redensifies it from within. It combines several ingredients with a hydrating and redensifying action.

- **Vitamin C** helps reduce tiredness and contributes to normal collagen formation.
- **Vitamin B8**, also known as biotin, helps you have beautiful skin, as well as beautiful nails and hair.

# How to use
**When:** in the morning.

Take 2 tablets a day with a large glass of water.

**Duration:** 1 month, renewable.

**Precautions**

Food supplements should be used as part of a healthy lifestyle, not as a substitute for a varied and balanced diet.

# Ingredients
Collagen hydrolysate (**fish**), calcium L-ascorbate (vitamin C), wheat seed (Triticum aestivum) lipid extracts standardised in ceramides and digalactosyl diglycerides (DGDG), anti-caking agent: silicon dioxide, melon fruit extract (Cucumis melo), D-biotin (vitamin B8). Contains fish.
