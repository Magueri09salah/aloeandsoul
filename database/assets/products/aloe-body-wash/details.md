# Description
With its very gentle formula, Aloe Body Wash cleanses and revitalises the skin while respecting its balance. Its fresh gel-emulsion texture melts onto the skin and leaves a captivating fragrance. Your skin is clean, soft and refreshed.

pH-neutral formula. Sulphate-free. Suitable for sensitive skin.

# Benefits
Helps soften the skin while boosting its hydration and softness.

Helps the skin retain moisture. Its key ingredient, aloe vera, helps fight the effects of ageing by supporting hydration, while vitamins A, C and E nourish and support healthy-looking skin.

# How to use
Apply to the body and lather gently with circular movements. Rinse well.

**Precautions**

For external use only. In case of contact with the eyes, rinse with clean water for several minutes.
