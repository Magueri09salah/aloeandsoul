# Summary
Aloe-Jojoba Conditioner leaves your hair soft and perfectly detangled, without weighing it down.

# Description
Conditioner is an essential step in any hair routine: it softens, detangles and nourishes the hair and adds shine. The new sulphate-free Aloe-Jojoba Conditioner formula also boosts the hydration of the hair and scalp. It is perfect for daily use and contains three different oils that lock in moisture without weighing the hair down.

# Benefits
- **Jojoba oil** is rich in natural fats that strengthen the hair and help it look thicker.
- **Argan oil**, known as "the liquid gold of Morocco", nourishes the hair with the antioxidant power of vitamin E.
- **Rosehip oil** keeps the hair soft and hydrated while strengthening it with vitamins C, E and B.

# How to use
**When:** after using Aloe-Jojoba Shampoo.

Apply Aloe-Jojoba Conditioner and leave on for 2 to 3 minutes. Rinse thoroughly and dry your hair.

**Precautions**

In case of contact with the eyes, rinse with clean water for several minutes. For external use only.
