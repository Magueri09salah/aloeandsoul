An exclusive 55-ingredient formula that combines the nutrients of the AOS Complex (Aloe OligoSaccharides) with vitamins, minerals and phytonutrients from fruit and vegetable extracts.

# Description
An exclusive 55-ingredient formula that combines the nutrients of the AOS Complex (Aloe OligoSaccharides) with B vitamins (B2, B5, B6, B9, B12) and vitamin C, which help reduce tiredness; minerals including copper, iron and manganese, which contribute to normal energy-yielding metabolism; and phytonutrients from fruit and vegetable extracts. The AOS Complex helps every cell in the body make the most of these nutrients.

# Benefits
A daily intake of essential nutrients is vital for the body to work well, yet most of us do not eat enough fruit and vegetables rich in vitamins and minerals. A supplement can help meet these nutritional needs.

**The AOS Complex**

The AOS Complex (Aloe OligoSaccharides) combines the polysaccharides of aloe vera with vitamins, minerals, amino acids and powerful antioxidants. Aloe vera is known to increase the bioavailability of nutrients. The blend works in two ways:

- It forms a protective layer in the intestine, so the body can absorb nutrients as well as possible.
- The amino acids in the complex wrap around the minerals to maximise their absorption, which can increase how much the body takes in by up to 4 times.

**Vitamins and minerals**

Forever Daily contains 12 essential vitamins that provide up to 100% of the reference intakes. They work in synergy with all the other ingredients, and their amounts are balanced to make the most of their actions.

Forever Daily also contains patented chelated minerals: minerals bound to protein molecules, which increases their bioavailability up to 20 times compared with simple minerals. They are carried directly into the blood, which improves their absorption, and they are designed not to compete with other nutrients, so the natural pH balance of the intestine is not disturbed and the minerals are better absorbed.

A patented molecular micro-encapsulation technology reduces the particle size of the nutrients: each molecule is naturally wrapped in a micro-capsule. This greatly improves the absorption of the nutrients, and some see their bioavailability increase up to 18 times.

**Roles of the vitamins and minerals recognised by the European Food Safety Authority (EFSA)**

- Vitamins B1, B2, B5, B6, B8, B12 and C, copper, iron, iodine, manganese: contribute to normal energy-yielding metabolism
- Vitamins A, B6, B9, B12, C and D, copper, iron, selenium, zinc: contribute to the normal function of the immune system
- Vitamins B1, B2, B3, B6, B8, B12 and C, copper, iodine: contribute to the normal function of the nervous system
- Vitamins B2, C and E, copper, manganese, zinc: contribute to the protection of cells from oxidative stress
- Vitamins B2, B3, B5, B6, B9, B12 and C, iron: contribute to the reduction of tiredness and fatigue
- Vitamins B9, B12 and D, iron, zinc: have a role in the process of cell division
- Iron, iodine, zinc: contribute to normal cognitive function
- Vitamins A and B2, zinc: contribute to the maintenance of normal vision
- Vitamins A and B2, iodine, zinc: contribute to the maintenance of normal skin

**FVX 20**

Eating a variety of colourful fruit and vegetables gives the body and its cells a wide range of nutrients. Forever Daily contains Forever FVX 20, a blend of 20 freeze-dried fruit and vegetable extracts rich in phytonutrients, which provide powerful antioxidants.

# How to use
**When:** every morning.

Take 2 tablets a day.

**Duration:** 1 month.

**Precautions**

Do not exceed the recommended daily dose. Food supplements should not replace a varied and balanced diet and a healthy lifestyle. Keep out of reach of young children. Not recommended for people with conditions that cause an abnormal build-up of iron (haemochromatosis, polycythaemia). Not recommended for children or for women who are pregnant, breastfeeding or wishing to become pregnant. Prolonged use is not recommended. Not recommended for people with thyroid disorders; people with hypothyroidism or on thyroid treatment should consult their doctor. Not recommended for people taking anticoagulant or blood-pressure treatment.
