# Summary
The perfect match of our aloe vera gel and the taste of a perfectly ripe mango, with sweet, juicy flesh.

# Description
The new Aloe Mango formula is the perfect match of our aloe vera (86%) and natural mango purée. The mangoes are harvested fully ripe to guarantee their flavour and their richness in nutrients, vitamins and antioxidants such as vitamin C. Aloe vera helps your body stimulate metabolism, support digestion and maintain the normal function of your immune system.

Now in a 100% recyclable PET bottle.

# Benefits
Stimulates metabolism, supports digestion and helps maintain the normal function of the immune system.

A delicious taste of fresh mango, mellow and lightly sweet.

# How to use
Take 40 ml diluted in 240 ml of water or juice, 3 times a day. Shake before use.

**Precautions**

Use as part of a healthy lifestyle, not as a substitute for a varied and balanced diet. After opening, keep in the fridge (7 °C maximum) and drink within 30 days.
